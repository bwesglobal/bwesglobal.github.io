Meet the Team

The people behind BOSS, Cargo Heating Management and everything else we build, at Blue Water House in Dehradun.

Our journey

From dairy farming to commodity trading to cargo heating management, and on to a full marine software-as-a-service provider: our journey has been challenging and exciting. Blue Water Trade Winds Pvt. Ltd. was founded by fellow mariners in 2006 for large-scale commodity trading. It took us almost two years to realize that trade winds help a vessel at sea, but not a company in Dehradun, at the foothills of the Himalayas, thousands of miles from the sea!

Scattered but still determined, in 2008 we built a product and service for optimizing cargo heating on board, and one of the leading tanker owners took it up. Within three years, most tanker owners and operators, including oil majors, were using our Cargo Heating Management (CHM) services.

Having found our strength in the marine domain, we took on a wide range of marine projects until 2015: dry-docking, PMS data transition and training on board, electronic oil record books, port captaincy, loss control and more.

In the fall of 2016 we started a project to capture the savings from virtual arrival. We soon learned that the industry needed a much broader system for optimizing and managing its fleets, so later that year we began an ambitious project: Blue Water Optimum Speed Services, or BOSS. Today BOSS has eighteen modules, built on our marine expertise and the latest AI and machine learning.
