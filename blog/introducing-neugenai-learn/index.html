Introducing NeugenAI Learn

Supporting the learning community and helping maritime companies develop their people in the age of AI.

6 October 2026 · 4 min read

Blue Water is pleased to introduce NeugenAI Learn, an online platform for hands-on courses, developed by NeugenAI with our support. Each course is a path of quests: practical pieces of work with clear goals, an AI tutor along the way, and assessment that looks at understanding as well as results.

We support it for two reasons: to give back to the wider learning community, and to give maritime companies a way to train their people for work that increasingly involves data and AI.

Why we are investing in learning

Through BOSS, AanyaX, and our work with NeugenAI, we help maritime organisations put data and AI to use. A tool is only as useful as the judgment of the person using it. That means knowing the subject, questioning what the tool says, and deciding what to do with it.

AI makes that judgment more important, and harder to see. When an assistant has helped with a piece of work, a good result alone does not show what the person understood. NeugenAI Learn is designed for this: learners practise with AI where it helps, practise without it where foundations matter, and show their understanding by explaining their decisions.

Giving back through education

Courses on NeugenAI Learn are built from quests, each with a defined goal, selected resources, and a practical outcome. An AI tutor gives hints and explanations in the context of the quest, in the learner’s preferred language. Work is assessed through tests, rubric reviews, and a short conversation in which learners explain their choices, and mentors review the cases that need human judgment.

The first course, Build and Ship an AI App, is for learners familiar with Python, basic Git, and the command line. Through Course Studio, educators and experts in other subjects can create courses of their own, so the platform is an invitation to teach as well as to learn.

Training for maritime companies

Much of a company’s most valuable knowledge sits with experienced colleagues and in existing documents. NeugenAI Learn lets companies turn that knowledge into training: courses on their own procedures, systems, and decisions, shared privately with a particular team.

With Course Studio, a company’s experts build the course together with their AI assistant. They set the objectives, the activities, and the standard a good answer has to meet, and try it all in a private preview before anyone else sees it. The course uses the company’s own terminology and priorities. The experts decide where AI assistance is useful and where people need to practise without it.

Activities can cover onboarding, reporting processes, interpreting operational information, or using digital tools. They can be written explanations, investigations, and practical projects, as well as programming where relevant.

A good place to start is one audience and a short list of learning objectives. From there, the course can be reviewed and refined with the experts responsible for it.

Get involved

Learners and educators: explore NeugenAI Learn, request a learner account, or request Course Studio access. Requests are reviewed before approval.

Maritime companies: contact Blue Water to discuss using NeugenAI Learn for your team’s learning and development.
