SIRE 2.0 readiness

Preparation for SIRE 2.0 inspections, built with oil majors, SIRE inspectors and industry leaders. AI does the trend analysis; your people make the calls.

Talk to us →

SIRE 2.0 asks more of every crew member, and it records how they answer. SIRE-SWITCH helps a fleet prepare for that: crews practise with self-assessments written for their role, the office sees where each vessel stands, and past inspection findings show where to focus next.

We built it with oil majors, SIRE inspectors and people across the industry. AI handles the analysis and the reminders; the judgement stays with your team.

Self-assessments for every role

Each crew member works through an assessment written for their rank and duties, and it adapts to the gaps it finds.

  • Guidance where it is needed: each question links to the relevant OCIMF guidance.
  • Readiness you can see: the office follows each vessel’s progress as assessments are completed.
  • Clear reports: results turn into an improvement plan for the vessel and the crew.

A databank of external inspections

Every external inspection across the fleet is archived in one place, and SIRE-SWITCH looks for the findings that keep coming back.

  • Recurring findings: trend analysis shows which issues repeat, and on which vessels.
  • Where to focus: the highest-risk areas are ranked for the next round of preparation.
  • One record: the whole fleet’s inspection history, open to the team at any time.

Dashboards for each role

Office staff and crews each see what matters to them.

  • Gaps against SIRE results: internal assessments are compared with actual inspection results, so the differences stand out.
  • Fleet readiness at a glance: compliance across the fleet, vessel by vessel.

Photos and documents

Certificates, records and photos are kept together and checked automatically.

  • Automatic checks: documents are checked as they are uploaded, and your team reviews anything flagged.
  • Works offline: upload and edit on board, even with a poor connection.
  • Reminders: the team is told before a document needs updating.

Why SIRE-SWITCH

  • Built with the people who inspect: shaped by oil majors and SIRE inspectors.
  • AI where it helps: trend analysis, checks and reminders run on their own.
  • People in charge: your team reviews, decides and acts.
  • Fits your routine: it works alongside the processes you already have.
