Optimum voyage planning is a smart first step. The savings are only real if the plan is followed, the data is right and the results are measured, so BOSS covers every stage of the voyage.

Plan

Weather routing and speed scheduling

The route optimiser explores every combination of course and speed against the forecast, within the charter party terms and safe navigation limits, to arrive on time with the least fuel.

  • Average fuel savings of 5–7%, measured against a transparent benchmark
  • Compare routes and speeds before you commit, and negotiate ETAs with the numbers
  • Boil-off-aware optimization for LNG carriers

How optimization works →LNG voyage optimization →

Execute

Every voyage, held to its plan

The live fleet map shows where every vessel is and what weather lies ahead. Behind it, BOSS compares each day's reported speed, RPM and fuel with the voyage plan, and alerts our operators the moment a vessel drifts outside the agreed tolerance.

  • Plan versus actual for every voyage, with alerts when a vessel drifts off plan
  • Emission control areas, reporting areas and ice limits on the map
  • Ask Aanya about any vessel or voyage, in plain language

Fleet and voyage management →

Monitor

Validated reports and vessel performance

MIRROR checks every noon report before it reaches a decision. The validated data then drives hull, engine and propeller trends, sea margin and warranty analysis across the fleet.

  • Errors caught at the source, on board, before they are sent
  • Hull cleaning, propeller polishing and dry-dock decisions backed by data
  • Charter party performance claims, built on validated reports

Performance and reporting →MIRROR noon reporting →

Save

Emissions and compliance, ready to report

CII ratings, EU ETS, UK ETS and FuelEU Maritime figures come straight from the same validated voyage data, so the reports are ready whenever they are due.

  • CII ratings today and against future targets, for every vessel
  • EU ETS allowances tracked, with EUA trading through Carbon Wallet
  • Sea Cargo Charter reporting, including split cargo parcels
